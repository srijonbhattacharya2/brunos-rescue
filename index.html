About Bruno’s Rescue

Bruno’s Rescue is a retro-inspired platforming adventure across dangerous worlds filled with traps, enemies, and boss fights.

Explore vibrant environments, survive challenging encounters, and battle powerful enemies in this indie platformer experience.
